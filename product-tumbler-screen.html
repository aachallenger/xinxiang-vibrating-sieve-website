Working principle

A tumbling movement distributes material across the screen. Residence time and material behavior are considered together during selection.

Configuration considerations

Selection begins with the material and the target result. Dimensions, contact materials, power supply and interfaces are reviewed as a complete configuration rather than chosen in isolation.

Information to confirm before a quotation

Final model and performanceConfirmed after application review; no fixed capacity is implied.

Installation & maintenance planning

Allow room for inspection, cleaning and replacement of wear parts. Provide inlet and outlet heights, access restrictions and the relationship to upstream and downstream equipment.

Before placing an order

Confirm the agreed equipment scope, drawings, material specification, electrical requirements and acceptance criteria in the quotation.
