BEFORE YOU ENQUIRE

A clearer brief.
A better starting point.

What information is needed to select a screen?

Include the material name, feed particle size, desired separation size, moisture content and target throughput. Photos or an existing process drawing can also help.

Which equipment is suitable for fine powders?

Rotary, tumbler and ultrasonic-assisted screening are options to discuss. Selection depends on the powder's behavior and the required separation; suitability should be confirmed using material information and testing.

Can equipment fit an existing production line?

Provide the available floor space, inlet and outlet heights, upstream equipment and collection method so the configuration can be reviewed.
