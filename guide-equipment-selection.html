Equipment Selection

A practical guide for your screening and material handling project.

Start with the separation

Define whether the task is removing oversize, separating multiple fractions or filtering solids from liquid. State the required output in measurable terms.

Describe the feed

Include particle distribution, moisture, bulk density and whether particles are fragile, sticky or irregular. A material name alone does not define its screening behavior.

Compare screening approaches

Review the equipment operating principles alongside your requirements. Ask for application review or a material trial before relying on an estimated capacity.

Check the interfaces

Record space, feed height, discharge points and power supply. Confirm access for cleaning and maintenance before finalizing the arrangement.
